Plan your day. Keep your data.

A calm, local-first dashboard for your tasks, calendar, money and people. It runs on your own computer, with no account and no cloud.

Download the latest release View on GitHub

Free and open source (MIT). Windows, macOS and Linux. Needs Node.js 20 or newer.

Everything for your day, on one page

Home and the morning brief

Today in a few sentences, the tasks that matter now, your schedule and its free stretches, the weather and your countdowns. Play my morning opens the day as a story, read aloud; Finish the day and the Weekly review close the loop.

Tasks

Streams, priorities, tags, subtasks, people, repeats and estimates. List or board, quick add in plain words, search with operators, a Bin, and undo everywhere.

Calendar

Month, week, day and agenda views with tasks, planned blocks and countdowns beside your events. Drag a task onto the week to plan it. Events come from Google Calendar through Claude, a read-only source, or a plain iCal link.

Finances

Connections > Money brings every bank and wallet into one place: Aureli (via Claude), Plasma One, Monzo directly, UK and EU banks through Enable Banking, or a CSV export with no connection at all. Every connector is read-only and credentials stay on your computer. The money brief tells you what is safe to spend.

Stories and animations with the live sky

Openings and scenes for places around the world that follow the real sky where you are: sun, moon, season and weather. Browse them all in a searchable gallery with dawn, day, dusk and night previews. Motion can be reduced or switched off.

The assistant and MCP

Ask in plain words. The optional Claude assistant proposes changes with a preview, and nothing changes until you press Apply. OpenDash also ships its own MCP server, so Claude Code, T3 Code or Claude Desktop can work with your dashboard, with the safety rails enforced by OpenDash.

Every screenshot shows invented demo data.

Local-first and private

  • Your data stays home. Plain files in one folder on your computer, with automatic backups. The server only listens on 127.0.0.1.
  • No account, no cloud. No analytics and no telemetry. Nothing is ever sent to the OpenDash project.
  • Only what you switch on. The network is used only by features you turn on, and only to reach the service they name.
  • Works offline. Tasks, the calendar view, Finances with CSV, people and the brief all work without a connection.

Quick start

  1. Download the zip from the latest release and unzip it. You need Node.js 20 or newer.
  2. Run start-opendash.bat on Windows, or sh start-opendash.sh on macOS and Linux.
  3. Open http://localhost:4173 in your browser (the launcher does it for you) and follow the short welcome.

Updates are built in: Settings > Updates checks GitHub for the newest release and installs it, verified and backed up.
