## 2.18.1

Garden (owner feedback: confusing, small, odd economy). Diagnosis: docs/dev/GARDEN_UX.md.

- The Garden is now a full-screen game: a top bar with level, coins, water, seeds, season and the next daily goal; big labelled tools on the left (Plant, Water, Harvest, Fertilise, Trim, Dig up, Decorate; keys 1 to 7) with a cursor for each; a seed tray under the garden; a side panel that can be hidden.
- A 'What to do next' card always suggests one step and rings the tile or plant to click.
- Hover a tile to see what your tool will do; hover a plant for a growth ring. Actions give feedback: floating '+2 XP', splash and darker soil, growth pops, harvested items fly into the basket, 'A hedgehog visited!' notes and a level-up card that names the new area.
- A skippable 6-step first-run tour (with a free seed) and a Garden guide behind the Help button; every top-bar item explains itself in one sentence; empty states say exactly what to do.
- Coins now come from time: focus-timer minutes (12 an hour for the first 2 hours, 6 after, none past 4) and a bonus for the time a finished task took. Quick, empty and repeat tasks pay nothing, sessions under 5 minutes do not count, each task pays once, and a day pays at most 120. Water and free seed packets follow the same minutes. 'Today: 42 coins from 1h40 focused (cap 120)' and a reason for every line are always visible.
- Seeds, decorations and tools cost about 2 to 3 times as much; garden daily tasks pay half. Nobody loses anything: earlier days keep their old amounts, balances, plants and decorations load unchanged.
- Golden harvests (now and then double) join the rare seeds and lucky finds.
- The Home garden card shows coins today, water left, what is ripe or thirsty and the next step, with Pick, Water and Open buttons.
- A friend's garden uses the same full-screen stage and bar, with Look and Water tools, hearts, reactions, gifts and the guestbook.
