# Play systems: my half of the hundred

The owner, 1 Nov 2026: "Garden features and game related, not necessarily dashboard." This half is things to DO: mini-games, what pets can do, cooking, long goals, collections, playing with friends, rewards, secrets that unfold through play. Land and its features are in GARDEN_PLAN.md, catching in CATCH_FEATURES.md, the world itself (places, visitors, weather, festivals, night sky, quiet spots) in HUNDRED_WORLD.md.

**The shape.** Everything is reached from one new place, Play, in the garden's menu, plus the things you place in the world. One row per game (`gardenPlayAdd`). Games pay fair tickets, not coins (so the coin economy stays as it is); tickets buy things from the prize stall, and every prize is a decoration you place, so friends see it on a live visit and on a saved copy with no new message. Nothing is gambled and nothing costs real money.

## How it is put together

- `src/app/94-garden-play-0kit.js`: the state, tickets, the Play panel, the prize stall, sound. `gardenPlayAdd(row)` adds a game.
- One file per game after that, `src/styles/94-garden-play.css`, and the decoration rows in `94-garden-0catalog.js` (the server checks decorations against that file).
- Hooks elsewhere are one line each: the menu row and the panel row, `gardenHudPaint`, the bubble on a placed decoration, `gardenCollectInfo` for eggs, water and walking speed for dishes.

## Saves

No new state keys and no sync change. Things you own are ledger lines (`k: 'pl'`, items `ticket`, `egg`, `dish:<id>`, `fos:<piece>`, `decor:<id>`), which add up across devices. Records and what you have found are one value each in `g.seen` under `pl.` (per key, latest wins). Old saves simply have none of them. Ids are stable.

## Economy

Tickets are capped at 30 a day, whatever you play, and XP from games at 20 a day. Games give no coins. Cooking never makes coins: dishes cannot be sold. The tune that rains works once a day and uses no water.
